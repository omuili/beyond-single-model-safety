# Beyond Single-Model Safety

**BASE Fellowship — AI Alignment Track**  
**Mentor:** Sijia Liu, Michigan State University & IBM  
**Status:** Work in progress

This repository contains research conducted as part of the BASE Fellowship project:

**Beyond Single-Model Safety: Exploring the Transferability of Machine Unlearning and AI Safety Across Models and Modalities**

The project asks a broad question:

> Can machine unlearning and AI safety transfer across models, modalities, and training stages?

The research examines whether forgetting and safety behaviors can transfer across foundation model families, including large language models and multimodal models, and how these properties behave through processes such as knowledge distillation.

The project follows three broad stages:

1. Benchmark development and transferability analysis.
2. Robust and invariant transfer algorithms.
3. Evaluation, analysis, and open-source release.

## Current Stage

The project is currently in the literature-review and research-design stage.

Research questions, experimental decisions, implementation, and results will be documented in this repository as the work progresses.
